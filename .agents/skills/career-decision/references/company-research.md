# Company Research

Resolve the actual entity first: legal employer, brand, parent/subsidiary, recruiter/vendor, and end client. Do not merge their claims.

When research is requested and available, use dated primary sources where possible. Record URL, publication/reporting period, access date, relevant findings, and limitations. Company announcements establish stated plans; role alignment and competitive advantage remain reasoned inferences.

Assess business/products/customers, strategic investment or retrenchment, observable competitive position, and the role's durability. Distinguish recurring ownership from time-limited delivery; neither company reputation nor a product label proves the role is core.

For public finances, compare reported revenue, profitability/cash flow, liquidity, and relevant debt periods. Distinguish accounting losses from cash burn. For private firms, use verified funding, actual runway statements, revenue signals, and sourced restructuring; funding amount alone does not establish runway. Unverified finances remain Unknown.

Separate employment terms from company finances. Record legal employer, end client, intended employment model, and evidence strength. Consulting in a company name or external stakeholders in a JD does not prove contracting. Explicit fixed-term/vendor wording establishes posting terms, not a signed contract. Verify signatory, reporting line, roadmap authority, renewal/conversion, and termination where decision-relevant.

If research is deferred or unavailable, say which applies and preserve Unknowns. Never imply a search occurred, invent URLs, or substitute confident financial/outsourcing labels for missing evidence. Give targeted next checks for material unknowns.
