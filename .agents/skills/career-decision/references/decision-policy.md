# Application Priority

Use classifications with reasons, not an uncalibrated aggregate score. Conditions and Critical gaps are non-compensatory.

| Class | Conditions | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| High Priority | No confirmed hard blocker or decision-changing unknown; all Critical capabilities Strong or credibly Transferable; differentiating Strong evidence; Expanded career value; acceptable known conditions and no confirmed High risk | Reputation or Optional strengths cannot establish eligibility |
| Selective | Actionable opportunity with manageable risk, partial support, or maintained career value | Explain why it is worth pursuing and why it is not High Priority |
| Low Priority | Confirmed user hard constraint, decisive nontransferable Critical gap, or evidenced goal conflict with little upside | Name the decisive blocker and source; silence in a resume is not a Gap |
| Insufficient Information | Missing/conflicting/unreadable information likely to change the decision | Identify what would resolve it; generic unpublished finances alone need not prevent a selective fit-based recommendation |

Apply this order: confirmed hard blocker; material unknowns; decisive Critical gap; High Priority conditions; other actionable opportunities. A confirmed hard blocker still dictates Low Priority even when other information is unknown. Possible outsourcing is not a confirmed blocker.

Record confirmed blockers, material unknowns, decisive requirement IDs, priority, rationale, why not higher, and next checks under the supplied contract. Use explicit current user goals/constraints; never import an example profile's preferences. Pending evidence limitations remain visible, and an initial recommendation is not a verified real-world opportunity.
