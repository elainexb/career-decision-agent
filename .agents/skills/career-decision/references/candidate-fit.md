# Fit Definitions

Compare complete work, personal responsibility, execution depth, and scope with the actual requirement. Fit and fact-confirmation status are separate dimensions.

| Class | Condition | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | Same kind of work, comparable responsibility/scope, supported key dimensions | Specific actions/results; tool names, titles, or acceptance criteria are insufficient |
| Transferable | Different context/tool with a supported reusable execution or decision mechanism | Explain the mechanism and remaining differences; related keywords are insufficient |
| Partial | Only some components, depth, or responsibility are supported | Identify supported and missing dimensions; preserve compound-requirement boundaries |
| Gap | Explicit lack or reviewed evidence establishing failure to meet the requirement | Cite the negative evidence; a missing resume mention is insufficient |
| Unknown | Insufficient evidence for a defensible judgment | Name the missing evidence and next check |

Strong, Transferable, and Partial cite concrete Evidence records and input version/hash. Pending facts can support a clearly limited Preliminary judgment; they cannot support a Personal approval. Lack of independent documentation does not by itself change the work's fit classification.

Critical gaps cannot be offset by Optional strengths. Skill declarations do not establish hands-on execution; internal collaboration does not establish external customer ownership. Calculate duration from supported employment intervals without double-counting overlap; unknown dates remain unknown. Fit conclusions are inferences even when underlying facts are approved.
