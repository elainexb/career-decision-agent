# Company and Role Risk

Classify Company, Employment, Ownership, and Longevity separately relative to stated user goals. Do not average them.

| Class | Conditions and evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Locatable sources support compatible conditions with no known material contradiction; state the evidence scope | Missing evidence cannot mean Low; JD roadmap ownership does not guarantee actual authority |
| Medium | Explicit manageable limitation or concrete transparency problem with a verification/mitigation path | Unconfirmed legal employer does not establish outsourcing |
| High | Reliable sources or explicit terms support serious goal conflict, a short opportunity window, or material risk; explain the mechanism | A fixed term is not automatically rejected and does not prove weak finances |
| Unknown | Insufficient sources, unresolved entity, or material contradiction | Budget-dependent renewal does not prove financial distress |

Company covers strategy/market/finance; Employment covers signatory/client/terms; Ownership covers decision responsibility; Longevity covers the work's duration and continuation conditions. Risk judgments are usually Supported Inference; quoted JD terms are facts about the posting. High Priority cannot conceal a confirmed High dimension. Decision-changing unknowns follow the decision policy.
