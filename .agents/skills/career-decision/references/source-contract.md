# Provenance

Every conclusion identifies its supporting sources and claim status:

- **Verified Fact:** directly established by an identifiable source, within that source's scope. A JD proves what it states; a resume proves a self-report, not independent employer verification.
- **Supported Inference:** a reasoned conclusion supported by cited facts; explain the inference and limits.
- **Hypothesis:** plausible but unresolved; state the verification needed.
- **Unknown:** insufficient, unreadable, missing, or contradictory information.

JD claims link exact quotes, jd_item_ids, and image/document anchors. Personal claims link Evidence record paths plus version/hash; formal IDs are optional until explicitly approved. Current company claims link actual retrieved sources, publication/reporting period, and access date. Preserve original-source provenance separately from analyst interpretation.

A URL's existence or a reference's validity does not prove the associated claim. Do not invent sources, approvals, or retrieval. An About page cannot establish profitability or a particular role's contract signatory. Use Unknown when evidence does not support the conclusion.
