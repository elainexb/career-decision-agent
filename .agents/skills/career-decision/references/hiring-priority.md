# Requirement Importance

| Class | Condition and evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Explicit mandatory gate, or capability indispensable to the stated primary mission, with JD anchors and reason | A generic Requirements heading is insufficient |
| Important | Consequential core responsibility or ownership without an explicit decisive gate | Frequency or position alone does not establish importance |
| Optional | Explicit preferred/bonus item or clearly supplementary capability | AI terminology or market popularity does not make it Critical |
| Unclear | Cropped, contradictory, or vague text prevents classification | Preserve the question; do not invent a threshold |

Each requirement retains requirement_id, original jd_item_ids, source links, exact quote, kind, importance, and rationale. Under the current analysis contract, kinds are capability or condition; conditions include role scope and employment terms. Split independent components while preserving their common original source. Contract duration is assessed as a condition, not a skill Gap.

Describe the role mission and evidence-supported hiring intent. Intent is usually Supported Inference or Hypothesis; a posting rarely establishes replacement versus new headcount. Product ownership requires what/why, prioritization, and success accountability; project delivery concerns scope, deadlines, and coordination. Do not collapse them into one responsibility.
