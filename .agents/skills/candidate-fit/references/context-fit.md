# Industry and scenario comparison

Use role_context from JD analysis and the single supplied Evidence document. Do not invent personal industry labels or maintain another profile. Cite JD items and actual contributions; bind the existing analysis/Evidence hashes.

| Class | Basis | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Directly related | Sources establish closely related sector or users/data/workflow/problem | Shared AI tools or terminology are insufficient |
| Adjacent transferable | Real context differs, but demonstrated mechanisms transfer; explain adaptation | Task-level overlap must not imply identical business context |
| Materially different | Sources establish consequential differences in sector or scenario | Missing experience does not prove a difference or an ability Gap |
| Unknown | Either side lacks enough context or sources conflict | May cite related work while keeping the overall comparison unresolved |

Industry and scenario can differ: a new sector may share a quality-control workflow. Explain what is established and what remains unknown. Known comparisons require locatable target and candidate evidence; Unknown target context cannot become a known similarity. Comparisons are inferences, never verified ability or an overall score.

Preserve pending/approved scope and personal/team responsibility. Context similarity neither upgrades capability support nor cancels Critical gaps or employment constraints. Do not rank execution below ownership. Career-decision applies explicitly supplied goals; no goal means no assumed preference for management, industry change or promotion.
