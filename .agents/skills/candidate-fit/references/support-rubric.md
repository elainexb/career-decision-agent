# Evidence support for readable reports

These axes describe support in the current Evidence, not independently measured ability. Keep the overall fit classification and decision policy; the display must not override a Critical gap or hard condition. Preserve requirement IDs, importance and exact quotes.

| Axis | High | Limited | Unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct support | Actual same-kind actions and comparable responsibility/depth support the full analyzed requirement | Cited direct contribution supports only part or a bounded scope; identify what it cannot establish | No defensible direct assessment from current materials; missing mention is not low ability |
| Transfer support | Cited work demonstrates a reusable mechanism covering the central requirement, with concrete adaptation and limits | A real reusable mechanism supports only part; identify the remaining scope/depth | The mechanism, source work, or required adaptation is unestablished |

High or Limited requires concrete Evidence references and a reason. Transfer also requires an actual mechanism and limits; shared terminology or hypothetical learning is not enough. When direct support is High, transfer may be Not assessed with no refs; state that transfer is unnecessary. Otherwise assess transfer. Do not mark generic product ownership as personal coding or technical execution.

A Strong overall fit requires High direct support. Transferable requires High transfer support. Partial may have High transfer support while direct components remain incomplete; explain this and preserve the remaining unknowns. Unknown overall fit cannot claim a known positive route; Gap cannot be cancelled by a transfer label. References and schema checks do not prove semantic support.

Report groups use this precedence: direct High -> Direct strength; otherwise transfer High -> Transfer route; otherwise both Limited -> Limited support; remaining cases -> Unresolved. A known transfer route can coexist with unknown direct experience; show both axes, not a false low-ability claim.

Count Critical+Important capability requirements by their fixed analyzed IDs. Display n/N and percentage; include Unresolved in N. Critical appears first and has its own counts. Optional, Unclear importance and conditions are separate. Do not deduplicate full source sentences: one sentence may contain multiple distinct requirements. Related repeated clauses remain traceable; percentages are counts, not workload weights, overall match scores or hiring probabilities.

Use concise analysis.interpretation in the main view. Full JD wording, source anchors, actual Evidence titles/STAR and confirmation state belong in expandable detail. Resume suggestions are distinct from missing evidence; no requested draft means no draft was generated, not that every suggestion lacks evidence.
